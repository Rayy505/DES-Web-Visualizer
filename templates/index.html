Understand DES,
one round at a time

Encrypt, decrypt, and trace the key schedule of the Data Encryption Standard — block by block, bit by bit.

Field note DES uses a 56-bit effective key — 256, or about 72 quadrillion possible keys. That was secure in the 1970s, but modern hardware can search the entire space in hours, which is why DES was retired in favor of AES.
